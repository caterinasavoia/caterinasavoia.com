Bio

I'm Caterina Savoia, an Italian tattoo artist currently based in Berlin, where I work across tattooing, visual design, and digital art.

Alongside my tattoo practice, I've developed experience in 2D and 3D design, creating digital models, visual concepts, and immersive projections. Throughout different creative projects, I've strengthened my skills in 3D modeling, light projection design, digital visualization, and graphic communication, combining artistic research with technical workflows.

I work across both traditional and digital media, always looking for new ways to merge illustration, spatial design, and storytelling into cohesive visual experiences.
