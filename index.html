Bio

Caterina Savoia is a product designer working at the intersection of function and material poetry.

Her practice spans industrial design, editorial objects, and spatial research — grounded in rigorous process and driven by a deep attentiveness to the sensory life of everyday things.

She is interested in the quiet relationships between people and the objects they inhabit, and how design can hold both utility and meaning at once.

Currently based in Italy.
